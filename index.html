FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

What is Base44?

Base44 is a vibe coding platform that lets you build apps and websites without the need for any coding experience. It turns your ideas into fully functional products, handling structure, design, and logic for you. Beyond building, Base44 offers Superagents — personal AI agents that automate workflows, manage data, and connect with your tools.

How does Base44 work?

Describe your idea in plain English — whether it's a general thought or a detailed requirement. Base44's AI interprets your instructions and generates the code, structure, design, and logic for your app or website. You can then refine and iterate by chatting with the AI until everything works the way you want.

How much does it cost to use Base44?

Base44 has a free tier so you can start building immediately without a credit card. The free plan includes monthly credits to generate and test your first apps and websites. Paid plans start at $20/month and unlock more credits, advanced features, and team capabilities.

Do I need coding experience to use Base44?

No. The platform is designed to be easily accessible to non-technical users. Just describe your software needs in plain language, and the AI will handle the technical implementation.

What types of applications can I build?

Base44 is versatile and can be used to build internal tools, productivity apps, customer portals, CRMs, reporting platforms, education and learning apps, health and wellness apps, and much more. You can explore examples in the Base44 Use Cases library.

Do I own the applications I create?

Definitely. All applications and content generated through Base44 belong entirely to you. Base44 makes no claims of ownership over anything you create using the platform. You're free to use, modify, distribute, or sell the generated applications however you see fit.
